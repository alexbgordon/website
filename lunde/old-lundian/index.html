    Lunde (Old Lundian)

    The old, formal register: verbs in three forms (samon, namon, exmon), tripartite case marking and base-twelve numbers.

    1. 01Lexicon

      Every word the engine knows: verbs, derived words, nouns, pronouns, articles, demonstratives and numbers.

    2. 02Grammar

      The reference: sounds, stress, sound rules, verbs, cases, adjectives, word formation and numbers.

    3. 03Verb conjugator

      Every verb conjugated by rule, with the derivation of each form. Type any verb to build its forms.

    4. 04Case conjugator

      Decline any noun, adjective or participle through every case, in the singular and both plurals.

    5. 05Number conjugator

      Any number from 0 to 248,831 as a Lunde number word, in base twelve, with its ordinal.
