Lundiyani verb conjugator (Modern Lundian)

Lundiyani verbs never change form: tense, aspect, mood and negation are separate particles before the verb. Pick a verb, then click any phrase to see how it was built. The rules are in the Lundiyani grammar.

  • subject
  • aspect tefri, talei, mai, kiena
  • negation lan
  • tense or mood lo, na, lei
  • lai, dei
