    Languages

    I've been creating these languages for a while. I started when I was little, and I was inventing languages before I could properly write in any of them. Lundiyani (Modern Lundian) is my most fully fledged one: it has the most words, and it's the one I have a lot of things in.

    And more are coming.

    Each language has its own lexicon, grammar and conjugators. Notebook pages for other invented languages are in the Gallery.
