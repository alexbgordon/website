    Languages

    Two invented languages, one old and one modern.

    Lunde (Old Lundian) is the old, formal register: its verbs come in three forms (samon, namon, exmon), its case marking is tripartite and it counts in base twelve. Lundiyani (Modern Lundian) is the modern form of Lunde: an isolating lingua franca, much later than Lunde and very different from it, whose verbs never change form.

    And more are coming.

    Each language has its own lexicon, grammar and conjugators. Pick a language above; every form on these pages is built by rule from the grammar. Notebook pages for other invented languages are in the Gallery.
