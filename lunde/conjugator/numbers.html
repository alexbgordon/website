Numbers

Numbers

Lunde counts in base twelve, which divides evenly by 2, 3, 4 and 6 and can be counted on one hand: the thumb touches the three segments of each finger.

  • Zero is hūł. An empty place inside a bigger number is simply left out: iğiōn-na (145).
  • ē- is used only when counting; before a noun it drops: ra zenen (twice).
  • Multiply: the multiplier goes first, joined (rasiōn, 24). Add: the smaller number follows after a dash (siōn-na, 13). Biggest power first.
  • After any number above one, the noun goes plural: ra osili (two eyes).
  • Ordinals are the number + the genitive, joined with dashes: na-de (first), siōn-ade (twelfth).
