    Lundiyani (Modern Lundian)

    The modern form of Lunde: an isolating lingua franca, much later than Lunde and very different from it.

      The Lundiyani word list: every word with its meaning and type, checked against the grammar.

      Particles, word order, nouns and numbers, questions, clauses and glossed example sentences. Also as a PDF.

    3. 03Verb conjugator

      Any verb with any combination of tense, aspect, mood and negation particles, checked against the grammar's examples.

      Any number from 0 to 248,831 in base twelve, with the Lunde word it comes from.
