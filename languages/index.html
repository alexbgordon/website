    Languages

    I've been creating these languages for a while. I started when I was little, and I was inventing languages before I could properly write in any of them. Lundiyani (Modern Lundian) is the most developed of them: it has the largest vocabulary, and it's the one I've made the most in.

    And more are coming.

    Each language has its own lexicon, grammar and conjugators. Notebook pages for other invented languages are in the Gallery.
